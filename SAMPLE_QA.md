### 3. What is the exit load of Groww Multicap Fund?

**Result:** Answered

> The exit load of Groww Multicap Fund is 1% if units are redeemed or switched out within 1 year from the date of allotment. There is no exit load (nil) after 1 year from the date of allotment.
>
> Source: [Scheme Information Document - Groww Multicap Fund (dated 30 Oct 2026)](https://assets-netstorage.growwmf.in/compliance_docs/Downloads/SID/Equity%20Funds/Groww%20Multicap%20Fund.pdf)

### 6. How do I download my capital-gains statement?

**Result:** Answered

> To download a capital-gains statement, use the Portfolio or Reports section of the platform you invested through, or the RTA websites. On CAMS (camsonline.com), go to Services for Investors > Statements > Capital Gain/Loss Statement; on KFintech (mfs.kfintech.com), go to Investor Statement and Report > Capital Gains Statement > Consolidated Capital Gains Statement.
>
> Source: [How to Get Capital Gain Statement for Mutual Funds?](https://www.mutualfundssahihai.com/en/how-get-capital-gain-statement-mutual-funds)  
> Last updated from sources: 08 Nov 2026

### 7. How do I download my ELSS tax statement on Groww?

**Result:** Answered

> To download your ELSS tax statement in the Groww app, go to your profile, select Reports, find 'Mutual Fund - ELSS statement' under Tax, and click Download. The password to open the report is your PAN in capital letters.
>
> Source: [What is a mutual funds ELSS report? (tax statement download)](https://groww.in/help/mutual-funds/order/how-to-download-tax-statement--for-elss--77)
