**Result:** Answered

On CAMS (camsonline.com) choose Statements > Capital Gain/Loss Statement; on KFintech (mfs.kfintech.com) go to Investor Statement and Report > Capital Gains Statement and pick the consolidated option.

> Source: [How to Get Capital Gain Statement for Mutual Funds?](https://www.mutualfundssahihai.com/en/how-get-capital-gain-statement-mutual-funds)
